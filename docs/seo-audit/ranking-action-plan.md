# Cinute InfoMedia — Ranking Action Plan (Off-Page / Authority)

_Date: 2026-06-04 · Owner-action playbook. This is the **real fix** for the 169 not-indexed pages._

## Why this matters most
CIM's **technical + on-page SEO is already strong** (clean robots/sitemap, unique per-page
canonical/metadata/JSON-LD, fabricated claims removed). The reason 169/183 pages are "not indexed"
is **NOT a code bug** — it's **low domain authority + crawl budget** on a young site with ~180 pages.
Google (and AI answer-engines like ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews) rank you on **third-party
signals** — reviews, citations, backlinks, mentions — **not** on what your own site claims about itself.

> So: code work is ~done. **This plan is where the ranking actually comes from.** It's ongoing, weeks-to-
> months work, mostly outside the codebase.

## 🔴 P0 — Do first (highest impact, this week)

### P0.1 Google Business Profile (GBP) — claim & fully complete
- Go to business.google.com → claim/create **"Cinute InfoMedia"** at the Mira Road address.
- Verify (postcard/phone/video as offered).
- Fill **100%**: primary category **"Website Designer"** or **"Marketing Agency"** + secondary
  categories (Software Company, Internet Marketing Service, Advertising Agency).
- Add hours, services list, service-area (Mumbai + "serves online globally"), description (use real
  capabilities, no fabricated numbers), and the website URL.
- Upload **15–20 real photos** (office, team, logo, work samples) — profiles with photos get far more calls.
- Post a weekly **GBP Update** (offer/news/blog link) to keep the profile active.
- **Why:** GBP is the #1 local-SEO + entity signal; also feeds Google's Knowledge Graph (helps AI engines
  recognize CIM as a real entity).

### P0.2 Verify GSC + GA4, submit sitemap, request indexing
- **GSC:** confirm `https://www.cinuteinfomedia.com/` property is verified; submit `…/sitemap.xml`.
- **GA4:** confirm the GA4 tag (`G-X378ZPPV30`, already installed) is receiving data.
- In GSC → URL Inspection → **Request Indexing** for the 10 hub pages + homepage + top blog posts.
- After deploying the audit-branch fixes, use GSC "Validate Fix" on the 404/redirect items.
- **Why:** ensures Google sees the fresh, cleaned site and re-evaluates indexing.

### P0.3 Seed Google reviews (target: 10+ in 30 days)
- Ask 8–12 genuine past/current clients to leave a Google review (send them the GBP review link).
- Aim for steady velocity (2–4/week), 4.5★+ average; **respond to every review** within 24–48h.
- **Why:** reviews are the single biggest "trust" signal AI engines and Google quote. (Note: this is the
  *honest* version of the fake review schema we removed in Phase 2 — real reviews, real schema later.)

## 🟡 P2 — Ongoing (months 2–3+, compounding)

### P2.1 Backlinks / digital PR
- Guest posts on marketing/dev blogs; HARO / Featured / Qwoted (respond to journalist queries);
  get listed in "best web development agency Mumbai / India" listicles (outreach to the authors).
- Original data/insights posts that others cite.
- **Why:** quality backlinks are the strongest off-page ranking factor; listicle inclusions are
  prime AI-engine citation surfaces.

### P2.2 Community presence (genuine, non-spammy)
- Answer real questions on **Reddit** (r/webdev, r/SEO, r/digital_marketing), **Quora**, LinkedIn,
  industry Slack/Discord — helpful answers, occasional relevant link.
- **Why:** AI engines surface community discussions; builds branded-search demand.

### P2.3 Topical authority content
- Pillar → cluster: deep guides that interlink (e.g. a "Web Development" pillar linking to each
  framework sub-page). Comparisons ("Next.js vs Astro for X"), buyer guides.
- **Why:** topical depth + internal linking helps Google value the deep silo pages.

### P2.4 Competitor / SERP keyword-gap research
- Pick 3–5 ranking competitors (Mumbai/India agencies). Use GSC + a tool (Ahrefs/SE Ranking/free
  alternatives) to find keywords they rank for and you don't → prioritize content/links there.
